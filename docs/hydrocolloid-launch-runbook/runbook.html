Setup guide

These are the click-by-click steps. Do each one on the day the calendar gives it, not all at once. You need the Day 1 logins before any of them will work. TikTok renames menus a few times a year. If a label is slightly different, pick the closest match. If a screen doesn’t match at all, send Harold a screenshot rather than guessing.

Setup 1. Connect TikTok Shop to Shopify (Day 2)

Result: TikTok Shop orders land in Shopify for shipping, and the TikTok pixel and Events API are installed.

  1. Shopify admin > Apps > search “TikTok” > install the official TikTok app.
  2. In the app: Connect TikTok Ads account (Ovena TikTok Ads login), then Connect TikTok Shop (Seller Center login). Approve both.
  3. Turn on Product sync and Order sync. Check the box that sends TikTok Shop orders into Shopify orders.
  4. Data sharing: set to Maximum. This installs the TikTok pixel and Events API.
  5. Shopify > Orders: confirm the filter “Sales channel: TikTok” exists. Ops ships these like any Shopify order.

Setup 2. List both rolls in TikTok Shop (Day 3)

  1. seller-us.tiktok.com (Seller Center) > Products > Manage Products.
  2. If the Shopify sync already pushed the rolls over, open each one and complete the fields below. If not, use Add New Product.
  3. Category: Health > Medical Supplies > Wound Care, or the closest wound care or first aid category offered.
  4. Product names:
    “Ovena Hydrocolloid Roll 2 in x 16 ft, Cut-to-Size Hydrocolloid Bandage”
    “Ovena Hydrocolloid Roll 2 in x 5 ft, Cut-to-Size Hydrocolloid Bandage”
  5. Images: at least 5, with the roll on white as the first. Use the Shopify photos.
  6. Description: paste the Shopify description. Delete any sentence that uses a word from the never-use list in Rules. Add no new claims.
  7. Price: $15.99 (16 ft) and $9.99 (5 ft). Stock: match Shopify.
  8. Shipping: Shipping template > Seller shipping > ships from the California address > handling time 3 business days.
  9. Attributes: fill in every required field. If TikTok asks for certification, upload the regulatory document Harold provides. Do not pick one yourself.
  10. Submit. Status shows Reviewing and usually clears in 1 to 2 days. Check Products > Manage Products daily. ESCALATE a rejection.

Setup 3. Create voucher HC10 (Day 3)

  1. Seller Center > Marketing > Promotions > Create > Voucher (or Product Discount).
  2. Type: Product voucher. Discount: 10% off. Applies to: both rolls. Audience: New customers. Claim limit: 1 per customer. Start today, no end date. Name: HC10.
  3. Save. It shows as a claimable coupon on the product page and in lives.

Setup 4. Open the affiliate plan (Day 6)

  1. Seller Center > Affiliate (or Affiliate Center) > Affiliate Plans > Open Plan > Create.
  2. Add both rolls. Commission rate: 20%. Plan name: Hydrocolloid Open 20.
  3. Free samples: on, 1 roll per creator, approval required, cap 50.
  4. Save. Sample requests appear under Affiliate > Samples.
  5. To invite a specific creator: Affiliate > Find Creators > search by handle > Invite (Targeted Plan, same 20%), or DM them the outreach message from Rules.

Setup 5. Post a video with the product tagged (Day 6 onward)

  1. Open the TikTok app, logged in as Ovena. Tap + > upload the file from the Cuts folder.
  2. Caption: one line with “10% off your first order in the cart” and no more than 3 hashtags (#hydrocolloid #skincare #tiktokshop).
  3. Add link > Product > choose the 16 ft roll. This puts the orange shopping cart on the video.
  4. Post. Share > Copy link, then paste the link into the Video tab.
  5. To make the video available as a Spark Ad later: on the video, tap > three dots > Ad settings > turn on Ad authorization. Copy the code into the Video tab.

Bio (set once): the Shopify hydrocolloid link with code TIKTOK10, for people who would rather buy on the website.

Setup 6. Go live with the Shop (Day 8, then every Wednesday)

  1. LIVE may need 1,000 followers. It may also be available to Shop-linked accounts, which Ovena is after Setup 1; TikTok’s current rule is unconfirmed. If LIVE is greyed out, ESCALATE.
  2. TikTok app > + > swipe to LIVE. Title: “16 ft hydrocolloid for $15.99, cut your own patches.”
  3. Shopping bag icon > Add products > select both rolls > Pin the 16 ft roll.
  4. Voucher icon > add HC10 so it shows on screen.
  5. Go LIVE. Every 5 minutes: cut a piece, stick it on, say the price, and point to the cart.
  6. After the live: Profile > LIVE Center > this live. Log viewers, orders and revenue in the Lives tab. Save the replay.

Setup 7. Paid campaigns on TikTok (Day 8 and Day 9)

  1. Live Shopping ad (Day 8): ads.tiktok.com > Campaign > Create > Product Sales > Live Shopping > the Ovena account > budget $20 > schedule it from 15 minutes before the live until the end of the live.
  2. GMV Max (Day 9): Campaign > Create > GMV Max, which is a tile on the create screen. If the tile isn’t there, go to Seller Center > Marketing > Ads. Type: Product GMV Max.
  3. Select the 16 ft roll. Target ROI: 2.0. Daily budget: $50. Creative: leave “all videos” selected so it uses account and affiliate videos.
  4. Submit, then change nothing for 7 days. Read results at Ads Manager > Campaigns, in the Cost, Orders and ROI columns.

TikTok’s “ROI” is gross revenue ÷ ad cost, the same number this document calls ROAS. If Ads Manager sends you into GMV Max when you try to create a Live Shopping ad or a Spark Ad for a Shop product, stop and ESCALATE. Don’t work around it.

Where the $7 and $10 come from

A Meta order with META10 brings in $14.39. Take off the roll’s landed cost of $3.20 (supplier invoice, delivered duty paid) and about $0.72 in card fees (estimate: 2.9% + $0.30). That leaves about $10.47 before outbound shipping. So $10 is the most a Meta order can cost before it loses money, and it assumes the buyer pays shipping. If Ovena pays shipping (about $4 to $5 a parcel, estimated), break-even drops to about $6, and Harold should lower both thresholds.

On TikTok, an order that came through an affiliate also pays 20% commission ($2.88) and TikTok’s referral fee (about 6%, to confirm in Seller Center). That leaves about $7.45 before shipping, a break-even ROI of about 1.9. The “no change” band of 1.5 to 2.0 can therefore lose money when most orders come through affiliates.

Rules

Words you can use: protects, cushions, keeps the area moist, waterproof, medical-grade hydrocolloid, cut to size, from Ovena Health, a US medical device company.

Words you never use in captions, replies, lives or ads: heals, cures, treats acne, clears skin, removes scars, any number of days to heal, “better than” a named brand, “Made in USA” or anything that implies the rolls are made in the US. Don’t mention the FDA at all unless Harold has approved the exact wording.

Images: no open wounds, blood or pus. Pimples and blisters shown covered are fine. No before-and-after framing on Meta; use “day 1 / day 3, still on.”

Affiliates: every creator video carries both #ad and TikTok’s paid-partnership toggle. Log every roll sent out.

Reviews on camera: real customer reviews only, read word for word.

Creator outreach message (DM or email):

Hi [name], I’m with Ovena Health, a US medical device company. We make a hydrocolloid roll (the same material as pimple patches, 16 ft for $15.99, cut your own). We’d love to send you a free roll to try for a TikTok Shop video, with 20% commission on every sale through your link. Interested? I’ll ship it this week.

Escalations

Send these to Harold the same day:

  • A listing or ad rejection on any platform.
  • A pixel that stops firing.
  • GMV Max ROI under 1.5 for 5 days, or any platform under 1.5 ROAS after $1,500 cumulative spend.
  • A video that passes 100k views, so budget can move fast.
  • A creator who asks for a flat fee.
  • An Ads Manager screen that forces GMV Max when you are creating a Spark or Live Shopping ad.
Injury, allergic reaction or any medical complaint

Do not reply, hide or delete it. Take a screenshot that shows the handle, date, platform and full text, and forward it to Harold the same day. Harold enters it in the company complaint file. That record is a medical-device requirement, so treat every one this way, however minor it looks.

Do not change budgets, targets or prices outside the Rules table without an OK from Harold.
